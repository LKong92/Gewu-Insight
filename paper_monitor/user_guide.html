Welcome to Gewu Insight

Gewu Insight is a local workspace for collecting papers, preserving their files and versions, and following journals, subjects, authors, and your own publications.

Why this workspace exists

Research rarely arrives as one clean collection. A paper may first appear in a feed, later acquire an arXiv version and a DOI, be downloaded under several filenames, reappear through an author's profile, and eventually become part of a project or your own publication record. Publisher pages, alerts, PDFs, folders, notes, and citation services each preserve only part of that history.

Gewu Insight is designed to keep those fragments connected. It treats discovery, identity, files, versions, organization, reading state, and provenance as parts of one continuing research process. The aim is not to accumulate the largest possible list. The aim is to preserve enough context that a useful paper remains understandable and usable months or years after it was first found.

Why the name 格物洞见

The name recalls the Chinese idea of 格物致知: approaching things closely enough to understand their underlying order. In this practical research setting, gewu—the investigation of things—means continuously examining the research object, its sources, authors, versions, files, relationships, and changing state.

Keep research objects visible, evidence traceable, and insight cumulative.让研究对象保持可见，让证据保持可追，让洞见能够积累。

Five design commitments

Local ownershipYour live database and managed research files remain in the local project. Online services contribute evidence; they do not own the workspace.
Visible evidencePDFs and attachments remain real files that Finder, Preview, and other applications can inspect.
Continuity over timeA preprint, later DOI, publication version, annotations, and personal contexts can accumulate around one persistent paper object.
Explicit state and provenanceCompleted, empty, partial, failed, downloaded, imported, and retried are different states. Sources and operational outcomes should remain visible.
Researcher controlAutomation assists discovery and maintenance, but classification, interpretation, organization, and scientific judgment remain yours.
Local-first does not mean offline-onlyThe durable workspace is local, while supported discovery, metadata, profile, and download features may require network access and remain subject to source, publisher, and institutional availability. Verify uncertain matches and versions; the app does not bypass access controls.

What this guide covers

This guide follows the work you do in the app: put papers into the Library, organize them, manage publication versions and attachments, review new literature, and keep the database and files safe. It describes the current interface rather than the history of the project.

Five areas of the app

  • Library — your managed papers, folders, spaces, versions, and related files.
  • My Publications — your publication list, citation metrics, author roles, and export tools.
  • Journal Monitor — papers published on a selected date, organized by journal and field.
  • Subject Monitor — topic-based discovery from supported online sources and feeds.
  • Author Monitor — monitored researchers, profile-linked discovery, and newly found work.

One continuous research loop

Important SQLite is the source of truth for Gewu Insight. Do not replace, rebuild, or edit the database by hand. Use the app to move, classify, or delete managed items.

Set Up Your First Session

Start with one paper, confirm its identity and context, then inspect or add a local file when the work is file-backed.

Choose your starting route

You do not need to prepare a perfect record before beginning. Start from the strongest evidence you already have, then let the paper object become richer over time.

Add and inspect a paper

  1. Open Library. Select an existing folder, or create one from the context menu.
  2. Add a paper. Drag a PDF into the folder, or open the target folder, Control-click blank space in its contents, choose Add Paper, and enter a DOI, arXiv identifier, supported URL, or title.
  3. Select the new item. Paper Details appears at the right. Gewu Insight attempts to identify the work. A local file is recorded only when you imported one or a supported download succeeded.
  4. Check DOI and arXiv. Enter a corrected DOI in its field and refresh it, or edit arXiv inline and press Return.
  5. Inspect the available evidence. If a local PDF exists, choose Open in Preview, or enter Paper Files and press Space for Quick Look. If no file exists yet, use Open Website or download a supported version when one is available.

The paper now has a cataloged paper record, a stable Gewu Key, and a Library placement. It may have no local file; that cataloged-only state is valid. A file resource exists only after a file is imported, downloaded, or attached.

Understand what may now exist

Verify the identity before enriching it

  1. Compare the title and authors with the PDF. A similar title is useful evidence but not sufficient proof.
  2. Check the DOI and arXiv fields. A work can have both. Correct an inaccurate identifier before refreshing more metadata or downloading another version.
  3. Read the source and journal information. Confirm that a publication record belongs to this work rather than a cited paper or a similarly named article.
  4. Keep uncertainty visible. It is safer to leave a field empty than to confirm a plausible but wrong identifier.

Complete the first Paper Files bundle

A cataloged-only paper may have no local file, and nothing is missing merely because Paper Files is empty. Continue with these steps after you import, download, or attach a file and the paper becomes file-backed.

  1. Open the Files section. It starts collapsed in Paper Details so long file lists do not dominate the inspector.
  2. Classify the available PDF. Use its small role menu to choose Published Version, Accepted Manuscript, Preprint, or another accurate type. Do not infer the role from the filename alone.
  3. Add related material if you have it. Add a supplement, annotated copy, note, presentation, data file, code file, or other attachment. Import first, then classify the resource.
  4. Check the current file. Open in Preview and the Library preview use the resolved current PDF. Selecting Use changes preference without renaming the resource.
  5. Reveal one file in Finder. Choose Show in Finder and confirm the real managed file exists where the database expects it.
Cataloged and organizedThe shared paper record and Library placement are durable even when no local file has been added.
File-backedAfter a file is added, its resource role and physical bytes can be inspected separately from paper identity and folder context.

Optional: verify shared identity

Copy the paper to a second Library folder, then select it there. The title, Gewu Key, Paper Files, and metadata should remain the same because the second folder contains another placement for the shared paper—not an unrelated duplicate. Delete only the extra placement when you are finished with the check.

Next useful actions

  • Use Download when a better publication version is available.
  • Open Files to classify imported PDFs or add notes, data, slides, and supplements.
  • Add the paper to another folder without creating a second independent work record.
  • Back up the project before a large import or migration.

Finish with a safe baseline

Your first session is complete when you can answer two questions for every paper: What is this work, and why is it in this folder? If the paper is file-backed, answer two more: Which file is current, and where are the real bytes? Paper Details, the Files section, and Finder should answer the applicable questions without contradicting one another.

Identity checkedThe title, authors, DOI, and arXiv evidence do not point to different works.
Placement understoodThe Library folder expresses personal organization, not a second scholarly identity.
Resource classified when presentIf the paper is file-backed, the current PDF and any attachments have roles that describe what they actually are.
File visible when presentIf the paper is file-backed, the expected managed file opens from the app and can be revealed in Finder.
Create the backup habit earlyBefore a major import, upgrade, or migration, quit Gewu Insight and back up both the SQLite database and the managed file roots. A database-only copy lacks some bytes; a files-only copy lacks the relationships and state that make the workspace intelligible.

Understand the System

See the System Architecture at a Glance

Gewu Insight is a local research system that coordinates structured state, visible files, and a personal workspace. It is not simply a web page placed in front of a folder.

How the parts cooperate

The macOS app window presents a local interface served from this project on your Mac. Network sources contribute evidence and downloadable material, but the durable workspace stays local. Closing a browser tab, losing a network connection, or restarting the app does not turn the online source into the owner of your research state.

What each persistent layer answers

LayerQuestions it answersWhat it does not decide alone
SQLiteWhat is this paper? Where is it referenced? Which resources belong to it? What happened in the last operation?It does not contain the bytes of your PDFs and attachments.
FilesystemWhich real files can Finder, Preview, or another application open?A filename or folder position alone does not define paper identity, version priority, or monitor membership.
Personal Research WorkspaceHow should the shared state be presented for discovery, reading, organization, and action?It is a projection of saved state, not a separate copy of the collection.
Persistent research workspace = structured SQLite state + visible research files, coordinated by the local application.

Why this separation matters

A paper can appear in a Monitor, the Library, a Space, and My Publications without becoming four unrelated papers. It can also acquire a DOI after first arriving as an arXiv preprint, or acquire a publication PDF while retaining earlier versions. The database preserves continuity across those changes; visible files preserve user ownership and inspectability.

TerminologyThis guide uses Personal Research Workspace for the on-screen projection and Visible Research Files for the Finder-visible assets. These are explanatory terms, not menu names. Together they form the visible workspace, but they serve different roles.

Understand the System

Understand the Persistent Paper Object

Gewu Insight treats a scholarly work as an object that persists through discovery, publication, file changes, and personal organization—not as a temporary search result or a single PDF.

One identity can accumulate evidence

The central record uses an internal paper identity. A DOI or arXiv identifier is important evidence attached to that identity, but neither is a safe universal primary key: an early preprint may have no DOI, an imported PDF may initially have no reliable identifier, and an incorrect identifier may later be corrected.

Do not collapse four different things

ObjectMeaningExample
Paper recordThe shared scholarly identity and normalized metadata.The work that later connects arXiv and a journal DOI.
Placement or relationWhy and where the paper appears in your workspace.A Library folder entry, a shortcut, a My Publications role, or an Author Monitor association.
Resource recordA described file or retrievable version belonging to the paper.Preprint v2, Version of Record, supplement, slide deck, or data file.
Physical fileThe bytes stored at a real path on disk.A PDF under data/Library or an associated attachment.
Paper record ≠ workspace placement ≠ resource record ≠ physical file.

What happens when the same paper is found again

Journal, Subject, and Author Monitor can encounter the same work from different directions. Identity resolution is designed to reuse one shared paper record and add source or context relationships. Copying a paper to another Library folder likewise creates another placement rather than a second independent identity. Its metadata, Gewu Key, versions, and attachments remain shared.

When the available evidence is insufficient or contradictory, separate candidates can remain until their identifiers or metadata are corrected. The shared-object model is the resolution target; it does not justify merging uncertain works merely because their titles look similar.

A network observation can be transient while acquisition is running. Once a result is cataloged as a shared paper record, it has a Gewu Key even if a particular Monitor view does not display that key. A Monitor result may still have no local Paper Files because no file has been downloaded or attached; adding it to Library creates a visible placement rather than inventing a new scholarly identity.

Gewu Insight also uses the same catalog and resource infrastructure for an imported standalone file whose scholarly identity is not yet known. In that case, the catalog record preserves the file and its relationships while later identification can enrich it.

Identity corrections should preserve continuity

Editing a DOI or arXiv identifier changes evidence on the existing paper. It should not silently make an unrelated second paper, rename every file, or discard your folder placements and read history. When evidence conflicts, inspect the title, authors, venue, and files before confirming the correction.

ImportantSimilar titles are not proof of identity, and matching filenames are not proof that two resources are the same. Use identifiers, metadata, file fingerprints, and provenance together.

Understand the System

Understand Why SQLite Is the Core

SQLite is the source of truth for Gewu Insight because it preserves the identity, relationships, resource registry, and operational memory that neither a filename nor an exported report can express.

Four families of structured state

The database does not store the PDF bytes themselves. It stores enough structured information to understand the files: which paper they belong to, what role and version they represent, where they are, where they came from, whether they are annotated, and which one should be used by default.

Why a folder scan is not an equivalent database

Finder can show that paper.pdf exists. It cannot reliably say whether it is a Version of Record or a preprint, whether the same work is also in My Publications, whether a Monitor refresh completed with zero results, which external source produced the metadata, or which attachment should survive deletion of one shortcut. Those facts live in SQLite.

Transactions, relationships, and WAL

Database mechanismUser-facing purpose
TransactionsKeep a multi-step state change together so a partly applied edit is less likely to become the visible truth.
Foreign-key relationshipsConnect a resource or placement to the correct shared paper instead of relying on a coincidentally similar filename.
WAL (write-ahead logging)Lets foreground reading and background updates coexist more safely than repeated full-file rewrites.
Schema migrationAdds new structures and backfills required state during an upgrade rather than asking the user to rebuild the database.

WAL improves normal concurrency; it is not a substitute for a coherent backup. Close Gewu Insight before copying its live database, and back up the managed files with it.

Journal subscriptions are durable SQLite state as well. The database preserves which journals are enabled, their identifiers and source settings, and their display order. Replacing or updating the product source directory therefore does not reset a user's Journal Monitor list.

Cross-layer changesSQLite transactions protect database state. A file move and its database update cannot be one native SQLite transaction, so the local service coordinates the steps and uses validation or compensating recovery when a filesystem operation fails.

Exports and provenance

JSON, HTML, Markdown, Word, LaTeX, and PDF outputs are derived artifacts. They can be regenerated, shared, or inspected, but editing them does not alter the source records. Raw source evidence may also be retained inside SQLite for provenance; this does not turn an external JSON export into a second source of truth.

Paper-level Monitor provenance

Journal Monitor stores more than a run-level success or failure. For every paper observed during a journal fetch, a normalized provenance relationship connects the canonical paper to the fetch run and the reporting source. The run supplies the journal, date window, status, and timing; the paper-level relationship records that this source observed this particular work.

Repeated observation of the same paper and source within one run updates the existing relationship instead of creating a duplicate. A later run remains separately traceable. Subject and Author Monitor relationships can point to that same canonical paper, so three discovery routes add context without manufacturing three scholarly identities.

Discovery is not storageA provenance relationship does not by itself create a Library placement or a local PDF. It records how the work entered view. Organization and file acquisition remain separate, explicit actions.

What the app does during an upgrade

On startup, the storage layer checks for required tables and fields, applies additive migrations, ensures required roots exist, and backfills stable keys or resource records where older data needs them. This is why a project upgrade should preserve the existing database and files rather than replace them with an empty database.

Do not edit the live database manuallyA seemingly small manual change can violate a relationship that is not visible in one table. Use the app for normal work and make a closed, complete backup before any diagnostic intervention.

Understand Visible Research Files

Your PDFs and attachments remain real local files. Gewu Insight adds a resource model around them so several versions and related materials can belong to one paper without hiding them inside an opaque cloud library.

One paper, one file bundle

Managed and linked resources

Storage modeMeaningSafe expectation
ManagedThe active stored path is inside the Gewu Insight project data area.Move, classify, replace, or delete it through the app so paths and relationships change together.
LinkedThe active stored path remains outside the managed project data area.Availability depends on that external path, and the app should not silently reorganize it.

A resource can be managed while retaining a separate external source path as provenance. The existence of that source path does not by itself make the managed copy a linked resource, and the external original remains protected.

Folder Loader is a migration-oriented workflow. It imports a visible collection as a collection instead of asking you to classify every incoming file as an attachment before identity resolution. The original source path and the managed result remain conceptually distinct.

How the current file is resolved

An explicit preferred publication-version PDF takes precedence when it still exists. Otherwise, usable publication versions are ranked so a Version of Record normally comes before First Published, Accepted Manuscript, preprint, and an unclassified publication PDF. A higher-ranked download should become current while the previous file moves into the associated bundle; it should not overwrite the old bytes in place.

Supplements and other attachments are never eligible to become current, even when the attachment itself is a PDF. This separates “a file that belongs to the paper” from “a publication version that can represent the paper in Preview.”

Published-version labelsFirst Published means the publisher's initially released file; Version of Record means the current formal record. The compact UI can present either under the broader label Published Version, while the resource registry retains the more specific role when it is known.

Fingerprints and annotations

When a PDF is imported, attached, or downloaded, its exact SHA-256, content-oriented fingerprint, annotation count, and annotated state are computed as the resource is registered. They do not depend on a later refresh. Exact SHA-256 answers “are these bytes identical?” Adding a highlight or note normally changes those bytes. The content fingerprint is designed to recognize the same underlying document while discounting annotation-only changes, allowing clean and annotated copies to be related without treating them as blindly interchangeable.

An exact duplicate can be reused instead of stored twice. A clean PDF and an annotated copy may have different exact hashes but the same content fingerprint; both can be retained when the annotation state is meaningful.

Why Finder and the UI do not look identical

The filesystem must expose practical paths and folders; the UI must expose the meaning of one paper bundle. A Library paper can therefore appear as a current file in the visible topic folder while its previous versions and attachments live under a mirrored Associated Files path. Paper Files deliberately groups those resources by role instead of showing every physical directory level.

ImportantDo not manually move or rename managed resources in Finder. The bytes may still exist, but the saved path and live workspace can disagree. Use an app-supported move, repair, or restore; do not assume a restart will rediscover an arbitrary manual move.

Understand the Personal Research Workspace

The five top-level areas are coordinated views over shared research objects. They are not five separate databases, and moving between them does not reset a paper's identity.

Views are relational projections

A Library folder answers “where do I organize this?” My Publications answers “how does this relate to my authorship?” Author Monitor answers “how does this relate to this researcher?” These are different relationships to the same possible work. The interface queries those relationships and projects the relevant controls, status, and files for the current task.

Library placements and shortcuts

A paper can have more than one Library placement. Copying it to another folder reuses the shared identity and resources. A shortcut tab is another entry point to a Library location, not another paper collection. A Space is an independent organizational root, but papers placed there can still reuse the same identity model.

Discovery does not require immediate file management

A Monitor result may remain a discovery relationship with metadata and status but no local Paper Files. The UI can hide the internal Gewu Key in a discovery context even when a shared paper record already exists. Downloading or attaching a file creates a resource relationship; adding the paper to Library creates a managed placement. Neither action should manufacture a second work merely because the user entered from another tab.

Why the same action can have different scope

Removing an author from Author Monitor removes that monitored-author relationship. Removing a shortcut removes an entry point. Moving a Library placement changes organization. Deleting a managed resource changes the paper's file bundle. The selected view supplies scope, while SQLite preserves what remains shared.

A useful mental modelThe UI shows a personal map of a shared object graph. It is “visible” because you can navigate and act on it, but SQLite—not pixels on the screen—preserves the graph.

Understand the System

Move from Discovery to Durable Memory

Gewu Insight connects monitoring, identity resolution, version handling, and personal organization into one repeatable research loop.

A paper can change without losing its history

  1. Acquire an early work. Subject Monitor finds an arXiv preprint. Its source and discovery context are stored.
  2. Identify the work. The title, authors, and arXiv identifier connect it to one shared paper identity.
  3. Resolve its first resource. A preprint PDF becomes current because no higher-priority version is available.
  4. Remember your context. You add it to a Library folder, read it, and attach notes or data.
  5. Acquire new evidence later. Journal Monitor or a metadata refresh finds the published DOI and Version of Record.
  6. Resolve without erasing. The publication PDF becomes current; the preprint and its provenance remain in Versions.

Operational state is part of scientific memory

A result count alone is ambiguous. The app distinguishes work that was never attempted from work that completed with no results, partially completed, failed, or entered a cooldown before retry. It also remembers imports, downloads, metadata refreshes, source tasks, and read state so the next action can be informed rather than blindly repeated.

StateMeaningReason to preserve it
Empty and completeThe requested source or date was checked and returned no matching records.Prevents “zero” from being mistaken for “not run.”
PartialSome sources completed while another did not.Allows a targeted retry instead of discarding useful completed work.
FailedAn operation produced a specific error.Makes the failure visible and avoids pretending the result is authoritative.
Cooldown or retry scheduledA remote service asked the app to wait or a transient error needs backoff.Reduces repeated traffic and protects normal foreground use.

Background work should not seize the foreground

Fetches, metadata refreshes, and imports report progress in reserved status areas and save their state. You should still be able to browse already stored material. Where callers share an online service, service-specific pacing, locks, cooldowns, and retry policies coordinate their requests. These controls reduce unnecessary traffic; they cannot guarantee that a publisher will never rate-limit or block a network address.

The loop is user-directedGewu Insight preserves the consequences of what you monitor, save, read, ignore, classify, and publish. It does not silently replace your research judgment with an invisible global keyword filter.

Understand State, Scope, and Safe Change

A safe local research workspace changes only the object and relationship you intended while preserving shared identity, resources, provenance, and recoverable state.

Eight invariants behind normal operations

One shared identityThe same scholarly work should not fragment merely because several sources or folders found it.
Many contexts, not blind copiesFolders, Spaces, monitors, and My Publications add relationships around the shared paper.
Deletion has scopeRemoving one relationship should not destroy unrelated relationships or shared resources.
State survives restartSaved reading and workflow state, retries, failures, versions, and provenance are durable.
Repeated operations convergeRefreshing, importing, or downloading the same evidence should not create endless duplicates.
Good metadata is protectedA weak or empty response should not silently erase stronger saved evidence.
Database and files agreeSaved resource paths should point to the intended real files after an app-managed move.
Failure remains inspectableDeletion, import, move, and network failure should preserve enough state to recover or retry.

Read an action by its scope

ActionIntended scopeWhat should remain
Remove an Author Monitor profileThe monitored-author relation and its profile context.Shared papers, Library placements, My Publications, and managed files.
Remove a shortcutThat fast entry point.The underlying Library folder and its papers.
Copy a paper to another folderAdd another placement.One shared paper identity and resource bundle.
Move a paper in the appChange its managed placement and corresponding stored paths.Gewu Key, metadata, versions, and associations.
Use another versionChange the preferred/current resource.The file's original name and the other saved versions.
Delete an associated fileThat resource relationship and its managed file, subject to confirmation.The paper identity and unrelated resources.

Recovery is part of the design

Recycle keeps ordinary removals recoverable. Import batches and operation status provide evidence about what ran. Resource paths, hashes, and provenance help diagnose missing or duplicate files. The app can migrate older storage structure forward, but migration is not a reason to discard the previous database or original files.

Back up one coherent research asset

A reliable backup contains the SQLite database and the managed file roots from the same closed application state. Copying only the database preserves descriptions without every artifact; copying only folders preserves bytes without all identities, relationships, classifications, and operation history. A portable desktop copy is useful for sharing visible folder material, but it is not automatically a complete live-workspace backup.

Before a major update or migrationQuit Gewu Insight, preserve the entire project data set, verify the backup can be read, then update the software in place. Do not create a fresh empty database and attempt to reconstruct the workspace from export files.

Understand the Library Layout

The Library uses a Finder-style column view, but the database—not the visible folder alone—defines the paper and its relationships.

Read the columns from left to right

  1. Source: the main Library, a shortcut, or a Space.
  2. Folders: each selection opens its children in the next column.
  3. Items: the papers and other files in the selected folder.
  4. Paper Files: shown only when a paper or managed file can expose related files.

Use the breadcrumb

The breadcrumb at the bottom shows the active path. Select a segment to return to that location. The List View / Table View control at the lower right changes the representation of items without moving them.

Folder Details

When you select a folder, the right side shows its name, type, and the number of child folders and items. This is informational; it is not another folder level.

Library

Choose Versions and the Current File

The current file is the preferred local representation of the paper. You can choose it manually or let Gewu Insight select it automatically.

Automatic selection

The automatic order favors a published version, then an accepted manuscript, then a preprint, then an imported or unclassified PDF. Within a role, a later recognized version normally wins. Availability and file validity are checked before a file becomes current.

Only publication versions can be current

Only publication-version PDFs are eligible for automatic or manual current selection. Published versions, accepted manuscripts, preprints, and an imported PDF classified as a paper version can represent the paper. Supplements, notes, images, data, code, presentations, and Other File attachments cannot become current—even if one of those attachments uses the PDF format.

If a Version of Record is already current, a preprint or accepted manuscript that arrives later is preserved under Other versions and does not automatically displace it. Manual preference remains available between eligible publication versions.

Choose a file manually

  1. Enter Paper Files or expand Files.
  2. Select a PDF under VERSIONS.
  3. Choose Use or Use as Current.

The selected PDF becomes current. Its existing filename is preserved.

Return to automatic selection

Control-click a Paper Files card and choose Use Automatic Selection. Gewu Insight evaluates the known roles again.

Classify PDF versions

Use the small type control beside a PDF label. Available labels include:

  • Published Version and Published Version · Annotated
  • Accepted Manuscript and Accepted Manuscript · Annotated
  • Preprint and Preprint · Annotated
  • Imported PDF and Imported PDF · Annotated
NoteChanging a type or choosing Use changes database metadata and preference. It does not rename the file merely to match the label. A current or preferred publication version must be replaced or returned to automatic selection before it can be reclassified as an attachment.

Download and Sync Versions

Downloads add a version to the paper bundle. They do not silently overwrite a different preserved PDF.

Download the best available paper

  1. Verify DOI and arXiv. Make sure the identifiers belong to the selected work.
  2. Choose Download at the top of Paper Details. Gewu Insight first tries the best available published file, then supported alternatives.
  3. Wait for the status message. The existing current file is preserved if the incoming file is a different version.
  4. Inspect Paper Files. The best version becomes current according to manual or automatic selection; older versions appear under VERSIONS.

Download arXiv files

  • Use the download icon in the arXiv row to retrieve the latest arXiv PDF.
  • Choose Sync Versions to retrieve all available arXiv revisions such as v1, v2, and v3.
  • Each revision is labeled with its arXiv version when known.

Duplicate protection

Gewu Insight computes exact SHA-256 and PDF content fingerprints as each new resource is registered. An exact duplicate is reused instead of stored twice. A clean PDF and a genuinely annotated copy can share a content fingerprint while retaining different exact hashes, annotation counts, and labels.

Validation before replacement

A response is not accepted merely because it begins with %PDF-. Structural PDF validation rejects empty files, HTML responses, truncated downloads, missing valid endings, and content that cannot be parsed as a PDF. A download is written to a temporary file and replaces the destination only after validation succeeds. If the request or stream fails, an existing valid file remains untouched and a partial file is not registered as a resource.

Roles follow the file that actually arrived

A publisher download is classified from the file and source actually returned. A Version of Record remains the automatic current file when a preprint or accepted manuscript arrives later. A publisher reference or accepted manuscript is preserved as an accepted manuscript rather than promoted merely because the paper also has a DOI.

If a download is skipped because the local bytes already exist, that skipped operation does not relabel the existing file from metadata or a newly discovered URL. Correct the file type explicitly if the saved role itself is wrong.

NoteA Monitor result is not forced into a Library placement. Where download is available, a local resource can belong to the cataloged paper even before you add that paper to Library or My Publications.

Import and Transfer

Back Up or Move to Another Mac

A reliable transfer preserves both the SQLite database and the managed file roots. The app bundle alone is not your library.

Create a full backup

  1. Quit Gewu Insight. Wait until background refresh or download work has stopped.
  2. Copy the complete project directory. This is the safest recovery unit because it keeps data, reports, config, the app source, and managed paths together.
  3. Preserve directory names and permissions. Do not separate SQLite from the managed file roots it describes.
  4. Store the backup separately. Use a second disk or a versioned backup location.

Back Up and Upgrade

  1. Quit the current version and make a full backup. Preserve SQLite and every managed file root together.
  2. Install or build the newer application. A normal software upgrade does not overwrite the existing data directory.
  3. Start the new version once. Required database schema changes run automatically against the existing SQLite database.
  4. Verify before deleting the backup. Open a current PDF, an older Paper Files version, an attachment, and a My Publications entry.
NoteSchema migration updates database structure; it is not a reason to rebuild the database or re-import the Library. Keep the pre-upgrade backup until the new version has been checked.

Move to another Mac

  1. Install or update Gewu Insight on the destination Mac.
  2. Keep the destination app closed.
  3. Restore the matching database and managed file roots together. Do not mix a new database with an unrelated old Associated Files tree.
  4. Start the app and test representative works. Open a paper, an older version, and an attachment; verify My Publications and one monitor.

Transfer only part of a Library

Use Make Copy Desktop on the source Mac, move the complete exported folder, then use Folder Loader on the destination Mac. Keep its verified manifest intact.

ImportantNever migrate by copying only paper_monitor.sqlite or only the visible Library folder. Either choice leaves half of the managed state behind.

Display naming and physical PDF renaming are separate choices. Review metadata before applying either one in bulk.

Choose the Library naming rule

Choose Setting > Naming Rule > Use Title or Use Citation. This changes the preferred managed display/name rule where applicable.

Configure PDF renaming

Open Setting > PDF Rename Settings. A template can use tokens such as:

  • {author} or {authors}
  • {year}
  • {title}
  • {journal}
  • {doi}

Rename one item from metadata

  1. Confirm its DOI, title, author, journal, and year.
  2. Control-click the item.
  3. Choose Auto rename from Metadata.
  4. Verify the result in the Library and Finder.
NoteChanging a Paper Files role or choosing Use does not rename that file. A rename occurs only through a naming or rename action.

Understand How Gewu Insight Stores Files

The visible Library stays simple: its main tree contains the preferred current file. Other versions and attachments live in a mirrored Associated Files area.

One work, several representations

Each managed paper has one database identity and a stable Gewu Key. DOI and arXiv are external identifiers attached to that identity; neither is used as the sole storage identity.

Where the files go

  • The best current PDF stays in the visible Library, Space, or My Publications tree.
  • Older PDF versions go to a mirrored Versions area.
  • Supporting information goes to Supplements.
  • Images, notes, presentations, data, code, and other attachments go to Files.

The associated bundle mirrors the paper’s managed path under data/Associated Files. When you move the paper in the UI, Gewu Insight moves the associated bundle and refreshes live paths.

Why the UI and Finder look different

The UI presents a paper as one item with Paper Files inside it. Finder shows the current file in the normal tree and the related bundle in Associated Files. This keeps ordinary folder browsing uncluttered while preserving every version.

Copies and filesystem shortcuts

When the same work appears in more than one Library folder, those locations can share one database record and one canonical managed file. Symbolic links can represent secondary filesystem locations. Make Copy Desktop resolves those shortcuts into real files for a portable export.

ImportantDo not manually move an Associated Files bundle or replace an alias in Finder. Use Gewu Insight so the database and physical paths change together.

Know What Stays Local

Gewu Insight runs a local interface and keeps its authoritative library database on your Mac. Network requests occur only for the features that need external metadata, pages, profiles, or files.

Local components

  • The UI server listens on the local loopback address, normally 127.0.0.1:8765.
  • SQLite stores papers, identities, relationships, settings, and operational state.
  • Managed PDFs, attachments, photos, and generated reports stay in local project directories.
  • JSON files are exports or diagnostics; they are not the authoritative database.

External requests

Metadata refresh, citation counts, journal/subject/author discovery, profile images, website opening, and downloads can contact their named services. The app paces services that require strict request limits.

Protect credentials

Optional service credentials belong only in Citation Settings or approved environment variables. They must not appear in a screenshot, support message, Git commit, or exported diagnostic file.

Handle Publisher and Network Access

Publishers can rate-limit shared VPN addresses or automated-looking traffic. Gewu Insight reduces overlap and request rate, but it cannot remove a publisher block.

If a publisher page is blocked

  1. Stop repeated refreshes. Additional requests can extend a temporary block.
  2. Try your direct connection. If the page works with VPN off, the VPN exit IP is likely the blocked client.
  3. Use Open Website or an external browser. Complete any normal institutional login or publisher interaction yourself.
  4. Download only when authorized. Add a legitimately obtained PDF with Add File.

Why figures may arrive later

Figure and article-page retrieval is queued behind discovery work and uses caching. This reduces simultaneous RSS, page, and image requests to the same publisher. A paper can appear before its representative image.

Service rate limits

Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar, and some publisher sources use shared pacing, cooldowns, or exponential backoff. Let the bottom status area settle before starting the same action again.

ImportantDo not attempt to bypass a paywall, CAPTCHA, browser verification, or access control. Use your authorized browser session or attach a file you obtained legitimately.

Understand Startup and Web Cache

The macOS app starts a local UI server, waits for the Library bootstrap, and then opens the main window.

Normal startup

The startup panel remains visible while the local service becomes ready and the first Library view is prepared. It may show more than one status message, but the panel should remain visually stable.

If the app cannot start

  1. Quit any old Gewu Insight window. An earlier local server may still own port 8765.
  2. Open the launcher log. Check logs/paper_monitor_launcher.log for the first error.
  3. Start the app again once. Avoid opening several copies while the first is still preparing.
  4. Preserve the database. Do not delete SQLite as a startup fix.

Clear a stale interface

Choose Setting > Clear Web Cache if the running window visibly uses old HTML, CSS, or JavaScript after an app update. This clears browser cache, not the SQLite database or managed PDFs.

Troubleshoot Common Problems

Start with the visible status and the selected work. Avoid broad repair actions until you know whether the problem is network, metadata, database state, or a local path.

A PDF cannot be found after a move

  1. Reselect the paper and try Show in Finder.
  2. If the move happened in Gewu Insight, close and reopen the item to refresh the view.
  3. If the file was moved manually in Finder, return it if possible, then organize it through the app.
  4. Check Recycle before importing another copy.

The wrong PDF is current

  1. Open Paper Files and verify every file type.
  2. Correct a mislabeled Published Version, Accepted Manuscript, Preprint, or annotated copy.
  3. Choose Use for the desired PDF, or return to Use Automatic Selection.

A duplicate appears after download

Compare file size and classification. New downloads use exact SHA-256 and PDF content fingerprints. A copy with annotations may be retained intentionally. If two rows are truly identical, keep the correct current file, then delete the non-current duplicate.

A monitor shows no papers

Read the source status. An empty successful source is not the same as a failed fetch. In Subject Monitor, retry only failed source tasks. In Journal Monitor, confirm the date and filters. If a VPN is involved, test a direct connection without repeatedly refreshing.

Folder Loader looks incomplete

Open the live or previous Loader log and the result guide. Confirm the metadata mode, source path, and any stop action. Re-running the same source should reuse existing files rather than overwrite the primary PDF.

A current file cannot be deleted

This is expected. Choose another version with Use first, or enable automatic selection, then delete the former non-current file.

ImportantBefore any manual filesystem repair, quit the app and make a full backup of the database and managed files together.

Storage Reference

These paths describe the managed project layout. They are useful for backup and diagnosis, not for routine manual reorganization.

  • data/paper_monitor.sqliteAuthoritative database for papers, identities, relationships, settings, and operational state.
  • data/LibraryVisible main Library root and preferred current files.
  • data/SpacesIndependent Space roots.
  • data/My PublicationsManaged local files for My Publications.
  • data/Associated FilesMirrored bundles for versions, supplements, and attachments.
  • data/pdfsLegacy managed PDF location retained for compatibility where needed.
  • reportsGenerated reading notes and reports. These are artifacts, not the database.
  • logs/paper_monitor_launcher.logmacOS launcher diagnostics when the local UI cannot start.

Associated Files layout

An associated bundle follows the managed scope and path, then a work-specific bundle. Its children separate Versions, Supplements, and Files. The exact physical name can change as the paper moves; the Gewu Key remains stable.

Database and exports

SQLite is the only source of truth. JSON, HTML, Markdown, Word, LaTeX, and PDF outputs are exports, diagnostics, or reading artifacts. Editing one of them does not edit the Library record.

ImportantDo not delete, rebuild, or “clean” the database because a generated JSON file looks stale. Diagnose the selected record and local path first.
